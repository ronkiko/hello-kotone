KOTONE — CHARACTER DESIGN PASSPORT
Production Canon for 2D Anime Side-Scrolling Platformer
Version: 1.0
Reference basis: master frontal character artwork supplied by the user

======================================================================
1. PURPOSE
======================================================================

This document defines the immutable visual canon of Kotone for all future
sprite, animation, atlas, pose, direction, attack, hurt, jump, idle and
locomotion generations.

The goal is not merely to generate visually similar images. The goal is to
preserve one and the same character across all frames and animation states.

Any generated frame must be treated as incorrect if it changes Kotone's:
- body proportions;
- silhouette;
- apparent height;
- head-to-body ratio;
- hair length or shape;
- sleeve length;
- vest construction;
- skirt length or construction;
- tights density;
- shoe design;
- colors;
- lighting intensity;
- shading style;
- line-art style;
- age impression;
- overall visual mass.

The character must remain recognizable as the same physical person even if
the face is hidden and only the body silhouette, clothing and proportions are
visible.

======================================================================
2. CORE IDENTITY
======================================================================

Character name:
Kotone

Visual role:
Young adult anime woman with a clean professional/office appearance.

Age impression:
Young adult woman. She must not read as a schoolgirl, child, teenager,
middle-aged woman, or chibi character.

General visual impression:
- feminine;
- neat;
- soft;
- friendly;
- composed;
- elegant;
- clean anime styling;
- not exaggeratedly sexualized;
- not chibi;
- not super-deformed;
- not realistic/live-action.

Primary design rule:
Kotone is a vertically oriented, slender, feminine character with stable
proportions and a clean symmetrical base design.

======================================================================
3. SYMMETRY POLICY
======================================================================

Kotone's base design must be symmetrical.

The following elements are explicitly removed from the canonical design:
- handbag;
- wristwatch;
- bracelets;
- side-specific jewelry;
- hand-held default accessories;
- any permanent accessory attached only to the left or right side.

Do not introduce new asymmetric details without explicit approval.

Left-facing and right-facing versions must be mirror-compatible in their base
design. The body, clothing, sleeves, legs, shoes, hair mass and default
accessories must not contain side-specific information that changes identity.

Allowed centered accessory:
- centered blue lanyard with a simple rectangular ID badge.

If the badge causes instability in a specific animation, it may later be
implemented as a separate sprite/overlay, but its canonical visual position is
centered on the torso.

======================================================================
4. SILHOUETTE
======================================================================

The silhouette is a critical identity feature.

Canonical silhouette:
- tall-looking and vertically stretched;
- slender rather than narrow or skeletal;
- moderate shoulder width;
- clearly defined waist;
- moderately wider hips than waist;
- long legs;
- compact torso relative to total height;
- small hands;
- small elegant feet;
- smooth feminine curves;
- no blocky geometry;
- no triangular body shape;
- no squat proportions.

The character must never become:
- broad-shouldered;
- box-shaped;
- triangular;
- squat;
- short-legged;
- overly thin;
- overly curvy;
- muscular;
- chibi-proportioned.

Across front, back, left, right and 3/4 views, the same underlying body volume
must be preserved.

======================================================================
5. BODY PROPORTIONS
======================================================================

Target proportion:
Approximately 7 to 7.5 heads tall.

This is a production ratio, not a strict anatomical measurement. The apparent
ratio must remain visually stable across all renders.

Head:
- medium anime head;
- not oversized;
- not tiny;
- same relative size in every animation.

Neck:
- slim;
- medium-short;
- elegant;
- never thick.

Shoulders:
- moderate width;
- softly sloped;
- not athletic or broad.

Torso:
- compact;
- feminine;
- stable ribcage width;
- no large change in bust or torso mass between frames.

Waist:
- clearly defined;
- narrow but natural;
- must not widen or shrink between animations.

Hips:
- moderately pronounced;
- wider than waist;
- stable width across directions.

Arms:
- slender;
- natural length;
- wrists remain narrow;
- hands remain small.

Legs:
- long;
- slender;
- visually important to the character's tall silhouette;
- thigh and calf mass must remain consistent.

Feet:
- small;
- elegant;
- never oversized.

Forbidden proportional drift:
- enlarging or shrinking the head;
- changing shoulder width;
- changing waist width;
- changing hip width;
- shortening or lengthening the legs;
- enlarging hands;
- enlarging feet;
- changing torso length;
- changing apparent overall height.

======================================================================
6. FACE
======================================================================

Face shape:
- soft oval;
- slightly rounded cheeks;
- narrow but not sharp chin;
- delicate jawline.

Eyes:
- large anime eyes;
- soft almond-like horizontal shape;
- pink-violet / lilac-pink irises;
- friendly and calm default gaze;
- stable eye size across all renders.

Eyebrows:
- fine;
- soft;
- natural;
- same general shape and thickness in all frames.

Nose:
- small;
- understated;
- anime-minimal.

Mouth:
- small;
- delicate;
- neutral or faintly friendly in default state.

Skin:
- light;
- warm-neutral;
- subtle soft blush may be present;
- no strong tanning;
- no dramatic hue changes.

Age consistency:
Do not make the face younger, more childlike, older, harsher, or more mature
between animation states.

======================================================================
7. HAIR
======================================================================

Hair is one of the strongest identity anchors.

Canonical hairstyle:
- straight bob haircut;
- approximately jaw-length to slightly below jaw level;
- compact overall volume;
- ends gently curve inward;
- full straight bangs;
- side strands frame the face;
- smooth rounded outer contour;
- no long rear hair mass;
- no ponytail;
- no braids;
- no curls.

Bangs:
- dense;
- straight;
- stable width and depth;
- must not become side-swept;
- must not split dramatically.

Hair volume:
- compact;
- soft;
- not spherical;
- not fluffy;
- not flat.

Hair color:
Pastel light pink.

Color behavior:
- base: soft pastel pink;
- highlight: lighter pearly pink;
- shadow: muted dusty rose/pink;
- never neon;
- never magenta-heavy;
- never peach-orange;
- never purple-dominant;
- never brownish.

Forbidden hair drift:
- changing haircut length;
- changing bob width;
- changing bang construction;
- changing hair saturation;
- adding curls or waves;
- making the hair significantly fluffier or flatter;
- changing the apparent hair density.

======================================================================
8. OUTFIT — CANONICAL CONSTRUCTION
======================================================================

The outfit is a fixed professional ensemble.

------------------------------
8.1 WHITE SHIRT
------------------------------

Type:
Classic office blouse/shirt.

Color:
Cool-neutral white.

Sleeves:
LONG sleeves only.

Sleeve rules:
- sleeves extend to the wrists;
- slightly loose fabric;
- visible cuffs;
- never short sleeves;
- never rolled sleeves;
- never elbow-length sleeves;
- never sleeveless.

Collar:
- classic pointed office collar;
- symmetrical;
- stable size.

Fit:
- neat;
- not skin-tight;
- not oversized.

Fabric impression:
Soft office shirt fabric.

------------------------------
8.2 VEST
------------------------------

Type:
Fitted professional vest.

Color:
Very dark blue-charcoal / navy-charcoal.

Construction:
- fitted at waist;
- V-shaped front opening;
- symmetrical;
- tailored;
- lower edge forms clean pointed/angled shapes around the waist.

Fit:
- emphasizes the waist;
- never boxy;
- never loose;
- never cropped differently between frames.

Canonical simplification:
Do not preserve any one-sided decorative pocket/stitch as an identity-critical
feature. The vest should be treated as visually symmetrical.

------------------------------
8.3 SKIRT
------------------------------

Type:
Fitted pencil skirt.

Color:
Same dark blue-charcoal family as the vest.

Length:
Above the knee, but clearly not an extreme mini skirt.

Fit:
- fitted;
- clean;
- stable;
- follows the hips without changing shape dramatically.

Forbidden skirt drift:
- changing into pleated skirt;
- changing into A-line skirt;
- changing into shorts;
- becoming knee-length;
- becoming very short;
- changing waist height;
- changing fabric color independently from the vest.

------------------------------
8.4 TIGHTS
------------------------------

Type:
Dark semi-transparent tights.

Appearance:
- dark;
- translucent enough to preserve leg form;
- smooth;
- refined;
- consistent density across all renders.

Do not change into:
- bare legs;
- opaque leggings;
- thigh-high stockings;
- knee socks;
- fishnets;
- gray tights;
- brown tights.

------------------------------
8.5 SHOES
------------------------------

Type:
Elegant dark pumps.

Shape:
- narrow / slightly pointed toe;
- moderate heel;
- compact;
- feminine;
- office appropriate.

Color:
Near-black or extremely dark blue-black.

Finish:
Slight restrained sheen.

Do not change into:
- boots;
- sneakers;
- flats;
- sandals;
- chunky heels;
- platform shoes.

======================================================================
9. LANYARD AND ID BADGE
======================================================================

Lanyard:
- centered;
- blue;
- thin;
- hangs symmetrically from the neck.

Badge:
- small horizontal rectangular office ID card;
- centered on the torso;
- simple visual design;
- no important readable text;
- no complex logos;
- no asymmetric attachment.

The badge must not jump from one side of the body to the other.

If physics animation is later added, its motion must be centered around the
same attachment point and must not alter the character's base silhouette.

======================================================================
10. COLOR PALETTE
======================================================================

The palette must remain stable across every animation.

Hair:
- pastel light pink;
- soft, slightly cool-neutral pink;
- moderate-low saturation.

Hair highlight:
- pale pearly pink.

Hair shadow:
- dusty muted rose.

Eyes:
- pink-violet / lilac-pink.

Skin:
- light warm-neutral skin;
- soft pink blush;
- gentle warm shadow.

Shirt:
- cool-neutral white;
- never yellow-white;
- never gray.

Vest:
- dark navy-charcoal.

Skirt:
- same navy-charcoal family as vest.

Tights:
- dark semi-transparent charcoal-black.

Shoes:
- near-black / blue-black.

Lanyard:
- medium saturated blue.

Color consistency rules:
- no hue drift between animations;
- no random saturation changes;
- no warm/cool shifts;
- no changing the vest from navy to pure black;
- no changing the hair from pastel pink to saturated pink;
- no changing tights opacity substantially.

======================================================================
11. LIGHTING
======================================================================

Canonical lighting:
- soft;
- neutral;
- mostly frontal;
- slightly upper-front;
- studio-like;
- consistent between renders.

Lighting intensity:
Moderate and stable.

Shadows:
- soft;
- controlled;
- not dramatic;
- no hard noir shadows;
- no strong rim lighting;
- no colored environmental lighting.

Highlights:
- restrained;
- soft;
- consistent.

The character must never become:
- significantly darker;
- significantly brighter;
- heavily backlit;
- orange-lit;
- blue-lit;
- strongly rim-lit;
- high-contrast;
- washed out.

======================================================================
12. RENDERING STYLE
======================================================================

Style:
Clean polished anime illustration suitable for conversion into a consistent
2D game sprite atlas.

Line art:
- clean;
- thin to medium;
- controlled;
- consistent thickness;
- not sketchy;
- not painterly;
- not pixel-art unless a later dedicated conversion step is requested.

Shading:
- soft anime shading;
- moderate detail;
- clean volume definition;
- smooth transitions with restrained cel-shading influence.

Do not change rendering style between:
- idle;
- walk;
- run;
- jump;
- fall;
- landing;
- attack;
- hurt;
- interaction;
- directional variants.

======================================================================
13. DEFAULT PHYSICAL POSTURE
======================================================================

Neutral posture:
- upright;
- relaxed;
- balanced;
- shoulders level;
- head centered;
- spine naturally vertical;
- no permanent hip tilt;
- no permanent asymmetrical stance.

Animation may temporarily deform the pose, but the underlying anatomy must
return to the same canonical body proportions.

======================================================================
14. DIRECTIONAL CONSISTENCY
======================================================================

Front, back, left and right views must represent the same 3D-equivalent body.

Right and left side views:
- base outfit must be mirror-compatible;
- body mass must be identical;
- hair length must match;
- sleeve length must match;
- skirt length must match;
- leg length must match;
- shoe size must match.

Do not allow:
- one direction to appear taller;
- one direction to appear heavier;
- one direction to have a larger chest;
- one direction to have longer hair;
- one direction to have shorter sleeves;
- one direction to have a shorter skirt;
- one direction to have different colors.

======================================================================
15. ANIMATION CONSISTENCY RULE
======================================================================

Every animation frame may change POSE, but must not change DESIGN.

Allowed to change:
- limb positions;
- body lean;
- hair motion;
- skirt deformation caused by motion;
- facial expression where animation requires it;
- temporary perspective compression caused by pose.

Not allowed to change:
- identity;
- body proportions;
- garment construction;
- garment baseline lengths;
- hairstyle construction;
- colors;
- lighting model;
- line-art style;
- shading model;
- age;
- apparent character scale.

Core rule:
POSE CHANGES.
CHARACTER DESIGN DOES NOT.

======================================================================
16. PRODUCTION SCALE CONSISTENCY
======================================================================

Within one atlas:
- all frames use the same character scale;
- neutral standing height is constant;
- ground contact baseline is constant where applicable;
- head scale is constant;
- line thickness is constant;
- rendering detail level is constant.

A walking frame must not be interpreted as a new character with a different
height or mass.

======================================================================
17. FORBIDDEN DESIGN DRIFT
======================================================================

Reject any generation containing one or more of the following:

BODY
- broader or narrower shoulders than canon;
- different waist width;
- different hip width;
- different bust volume;
- different leg length;
- different arm length;
- different head size;
- different apparent height.

HAIR
- different bob length;
- different bang shape;
- substantially different hair volume;
- different pink hue;
- new hair ornaments.

CLOTHING
- short sleeves;
- rolled sleeves;
- missing cuffs;
- different collar;
- different vest cut;
- asymmetric vest detailing;
- different skirt length;
- different skirt type;
- bare legs;
- opaque leggings;
- different shoe class.

ACCESSORIES
- handbag;
- watch;
- bracelet;
- random jewelry;
- side pouch;
- belt accessories;
- side-specific decorations.

COLOR
- saturated hot-pink hair;
- purple hair;
- orange hair;
- black shirt;
- gray shirt;
- pure black vest when previous frames are navy-charcoal;
- inconsistent tights opacity.

RENDER
- cel-shading suddenly becoming hard;
- painterly rendering;
- 3D CGI rendering;
- chibi styling;
- semi-realistic face;
- different line thickness;
- dramatically different lighting.

======================================================================
19. MASTER REFERENCE PRIORITY
======================================================================

The supplied frontal artwork is the primary visual source of truth for:
- face;
- hairstyle;
- body type;
- body proportions;
- outfit;
- skirt length;
- tights;
- shoes;
- palette;
- lighting;
- rendering style;
- overall detail level.

The only deliberate canonical changes from the master artwork are:
1. remove the handbag;
2. remove the wristwatch;
3. avoid any unnecessary left/right-specific clothing detail;
4. enforce a symmetrical base character design.

If a future generated image conflicts with this passport, this passport and
the master artwork take priority over the generated variation.

======================================================================
20. ONE-SENTENCE CANON
======================================================================

Kotone is a young adult, slender, long-legged anime office woman with a soft
oval face, large pink-violet eyes, a compact pastel-pink straight bob with full
bangs, a white long-sleeve office shirt, fitted dark navy-charcoal vest,
matching fitted pencil skirt above the knee, dark semi-transparent tights,
dark elegant pumps and an optional centered blue lanyard with a simple ID
badge, rendered with the same soft neutral studio lighting, palette,
proportions and anime shading in every frame, with no handbag, no wristwatch
and no asymmetric permanent accessories.
